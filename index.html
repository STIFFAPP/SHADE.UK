ENGINEERED, NOT IMPROVISED

A replaceable skin on a properly designed structure.

Our development concept combines a galvanised and powder-coated steel frame with an architectural tensile membrane and replaceable keder-style attachment. Final structural members, connections, anchors and foundations are determined by engineering for the product and site.
