HOW IT'S BUILT

Designed as a proper structure.

The working concept uses a galvanised/powder-coated steel frame and a replaceable architectural membrane. Final member sizes, foundations, anchors and connections must be confirmed by structural engineering for the actual site.

Concept column120 × 120 × 5 mm SHS
Concept primary arm100 × 50 × 5 mm RHS
Concept secondary100 × 50 × 5 mm RHS
Concept cross member50 × 50 × 3 mm RHS
MembranePVC/PVDF tensile membrane
Attachment conceptReplaceable keder rail system

Concept specifications only — not fabrication or installation instructions. Site-specific structural design and approval are required.
