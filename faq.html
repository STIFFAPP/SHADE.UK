FAQs

Before you order.

The practical questions about products, planning, installation, relocation and care.

Is SHADE ONE a permanent garage?

No. It is an open-sided architectural canopy concept intended to provide vehicle weather protection while keeping the driveway visually and physically open.

Will I need planning permission?

Planning depends on the property, location, dimensions, position, listed/conservation status and other site-specific factors. We will not describe every canopy as automatically permitted development. The position should be checked before fabrication or installation.

What protects the steel?

The development specification is based around hot-dip galvanising plus an exterior powder-coated architectural finish, subject to the final approved fabrication specification.

Can the fabric be replaced?

Replaceability is a core design objective. The current concept uses a keder/luff-style track and engineered tensioning arrangement so a membrane can be renewed without replacing the whole steel structure.

Can I move the canopy if I move house?

Qualifying launch systems may include MOVE ONCE™ relocation labour within the stated promotional terms. New foundations, site engineering, abnormal access, transport outside the agreed area and modifications are separate costs.

What is SHADE CARE?

An optional annual service concept covering canopy washing, gutter/drainage cleaning and a visible condition inspection. Replacement parts and remedial work would be quoted separately.

Are the website prices final?

No. They are indicative starting figures for budgeting. The final quotation depends on survey, final system specification, structural requirements, ground conditions, access, options and any planning or project requirements.

How long does installation take?

The programme depends heavily on groundworks, concrete curing, access, system size and weather. A project programme is provided once the site and final design are known.

Can I add lights, screens or custom graphics?

Yes, depending on the product. Lighting provision, drainage, side screens, selected RAL colours and graphics are part of the planned options range.

What warranty do I get?

Final warranty terms will be issued with the production specification and quotation. Until those supplier and installation terms are finalised, the website does not promise a universal warranty duration.

Do you offer finance?

Not currently advertised. Finance should only be added once an appropriate regulated/authorised finance arrangement is in place.
